Hydrodynamic Focusing

The needle feeds sample into the cone, where cells arrive spread across the stream and clumped. The physical cone narrows the whole fluid column, which speeds the cells up and squeezes them to a fine point, single file. Focusing is only this geometric act: it does not order the flow, so past the nozzle the stream can still turn turbulent. Keeping it smooth is a separate matter, laminar flow, step 2.
