Laminar Flow

In a narrow channel the fluid moves in smooth parallel layers that slide past one another and never mix. The speed is not uniform: it is fastest along the centre line and falls to zero at the walls, a parabolic profile. A particle rides its own layer at that layer's speed and stays in its lane, which is why a marked stream stays a clean thread instead of blurring.
