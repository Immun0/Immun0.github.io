Hydrodynamic Focusing and Laminar Flow: Stabilising Each Other

The same cells, the same laser, three conditions at once. Hydrodynamic focusing without laminar flow wobbles and misses. Laminar flow without focusing spreads the cells across layers. Only both together hold every cell on the centre line so it meets the sweet spot the same way. The gauge on the right records where recent cells actually crossed the laser.
