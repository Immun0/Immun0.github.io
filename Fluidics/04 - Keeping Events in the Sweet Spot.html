Keeping Events in the Sweet Spot

Focusing and laminar flow together: the sample runs as a central core inside a fast sheath that flows in parallel layers, fastest at the centre and slowest at the walls, and never mixes. As the channel narrows the core is squeezed until it is one cell wide, so cells line up single file, and elongated cells rotate until their long axis points along the flow. Single file and aligned, each cell meets the focused laser sweet spot the same way.
