Speed vs Resolution

Slow and fast, side by side. The sheath wraps the sample and the cone focuses it to a core. Run slow and the core stays tight, so every cell crosses the uniform centre of the laser and the CV is tight. Run fast for throughput and the core swells inside the sheath, so cells drift off the beam centre and the CV climbs. Watch the two histograms fill together.
