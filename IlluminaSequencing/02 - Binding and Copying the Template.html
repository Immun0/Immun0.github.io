Binding and Copying the Template

The flow cell surface carries short complementary oligos anchored across its surface. Each DNA fragment in solution has adapter sequences on both ends. One adapter end hybridises to its complementary surface oligo, tethering the fragment. Polymerase then copies from that anchor all the way to the other end. The original template washes away. The copy stays bound.
