Building a Cluster

One long, anchored strand folds in half — its adapter reaches over and binds a matching primer nearby. That adapter-to-primer contact is the bridge. Polymerase extends along it, copying the whole fragment, and the bridge separates back into two straight, independent copies. Every one of those folds over again. Doubling each round, this is what turns one fragment into a dense, detectable cluster.
