Binding and Copying the Template

A DNA fragment lands on the flow cell and binds a short primer fixed to the surface. Every fragment has known adapter sequences on both ends, which is what lets it bind at all — the middle is the actual DNA being sequenced. Polymerase copies the whole thing. The copy stays bound, the original is washed away. No color here yet — that only comes in once sequencing itself starts.
