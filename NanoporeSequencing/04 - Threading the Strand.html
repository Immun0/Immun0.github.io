Threading the Strand

A prepared molecule docks on a pore. The motor unwinds the duplex and ratchets one strand through, base by base, while the displaced strand loops above. Each base resists the ion flow differently, so the current settles at one of four levels.
Schematic: in reality a short stretch of bases sits in the sensing region at once, so the signal reflects several bases, not one.
