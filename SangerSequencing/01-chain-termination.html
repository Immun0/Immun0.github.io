Chain Termination

Many copies of the same primed template extend at once. Almost every time, a normal base gets added and the strand keeps growing. At a random position in each copy, a fluorescently labelled terminator base gets incorporated instead: it blocks any further extension. In a real reaction, millions of copies guarantee every possible length gets covered; here, 25 copies are shown terminating at every length from 1 to 25, once each, so nothing is missing once they're sorted by size.
Every copy starts extending from the same primer
