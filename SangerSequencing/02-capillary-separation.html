Capillary Separation

All 25 terminated fragments enter the capillary together, still unsorted by size. Under the same electric field, shorter fragments migrate faster than longer ones, so the mixture spreads out as it travels. A detector near the far end reads each fragment's colour the instant it passes: that single event both adds a letter to the sequence and plots a peak on the trace below, shortest to longest.
