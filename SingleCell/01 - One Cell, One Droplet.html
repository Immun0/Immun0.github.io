One Cell, One Droplet

Oil flows straight through the main channel. Cells drop in from above and barcoded gel beads rise from below, meeting the oil at the junction where it shears them into sealed droplets. Each gel bead carries its own barcode, shown here as its own stripe pattern. Cells are loaded sparse, so most droplets are empty and few hold two: the deliberate price of never mixing two cells in one droplet.
