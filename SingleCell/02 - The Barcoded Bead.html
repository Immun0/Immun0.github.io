The Barcoded Bead

A gel bead is "barcoded" because every one of the millions of oligos coating it carries the same barcode, unique to that bead, shown here as its stripe pattern. Zoom into one oligo and it has four parts: a sequencing adapter, the cell barcode (the same stripe pattern across this bead), a UMI or unique molecular identifier (different on every oligo), and a poly(dT) tail to catch the cell's mRNA. The barcode says which cell the read came from; the UMI says which individual molecule, so PCR duplicates can be counted out later.
