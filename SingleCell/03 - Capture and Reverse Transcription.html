Capture and Reverse Transcription

Inside the sealed droplet, the gel bead dissolves and releases its barcoded oligos, and the cell lyses and spills its mRNA. Each mRNA's poly(A) tail pairs with an oligo's poly(dT), and reverse transcription copies the mRNA into cDNA. That cDNA now carries the cell barcode and a UMI, so every molecule from this cell walks away tagged with the same barcode.
