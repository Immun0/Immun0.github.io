Pool and Trace Back

Every droplet now holds one cell's cDNA, tagged with that cell's barcode. Break the emulsion and it all pours into one tube, jumbled together, then sequenced in bulk. Nothing is lost, because software reads each molecule's barcode and sorts every read back to the cell it came from. One tube in, per-cell data out: the whole point of the barcode.
